What your site is costing you — Rock N Beauty

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a Boulder City client looking for a color or bridal stylist would: search, open a result, check the work and the stylist, book. Your address, hours note and the call-or-text number are easy to find, and the look fits the shop. The leaks are very heavy pages, no way to book from the site, two phone numbers floating around the web and a sitemap that points at another address.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your gallery page loads about 47 MB.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the gallery page loaded about 47 MB, the main content showed at 18.5 and 10.8 seconds and the speed score was 60 and 67 out of 100. Three photos are about 10 MB each, and the page shows 1 photo per category. The test estimated that sizing the images properly would save about 42 MB. One test setup, two runs per page; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your homepage loads about 4.9 MB and shows its main content late.

The homepage hero picture is a 2.9 MB PNG and the logo is a 1.4 MB PNG. In the same test the main content showed at 14.9 and 25.0 seconds (speed score 60 and 58), the Stylists page loaded about 5.3 MB, with a layout shift of 0.181 on one of its two runs, and the Contact page about 2.3 MB (speed score 65 on both runs).

03

Visitors can't book from the site.

Every Book Appointment button on the pages I saved goes to the Stylists page, which has a phone link and no booking link. Your Services FAQ says Alesia and Caitlynn use RingMyStylist for online booking, but no page I saved links to RingMyStylist. The only ways to book on the site are call, text or the contact form.

04

Two phone numbers for the same shop.

Your site lists (725) 245-9250 on every page. Your RingMyStylist profile, the Boulder City Chamber of Commerce directory and your Nextdoor page list 702-883-6375. A search for a bridal stylist quoted the 702 number, from RingMyStylist. I didn't dial either, so I can't say which one is current.

05

Your sitemap points to a different website.

Your robots.txt and your sitemap.xml list pages on rock-n-beauty-hub.lovable.app instead of rocknbeautybc.com, so a crawler following them is sent to the builder's preview address. The page address tags (canonical and og:url) are written as a bare “/” on 8 of the 9 pages I opened, and the Lighthouse test flagged the canonical as invalid on all four pages I tested.

06

The business markup uses an address that doesn't load.

The markup on every page names www.rocknbeautybc.com as the shop's address, but when I opened https://www.rocknbeautybc.com/ it failed with a secure-connection error. Without the www, the site loads normally.

07

A builder badge and a stale review date are on the page.

The pages show a floating “Edit with Lovable” badge that links to the builder's project page. The homepage review block shows “1 week ago” next to a review, beside others dated 5/2026, 2021 and August 2026, and I can't tell whether that label updates.

What's working

Your address, parking note and “by appointment only” hours are in plain text on the Contact page, and your phone is a tap-to-call link and your contact page has a text link. Every page has its own title and description, every image has alt text, and your homepage shows 4.8 stars from 85 Google reviews and the 2026 Best of Boulder City first-place award. The accessibility score was 100 on the Contact, Gallery and Stylists pages and 93 on the homepage. Your robots.txt doesn't block AI crawlers, the pages are readable without scripts, you publish business markup with your address, map position, hours and rating, and you link Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks on a phone (untested), whether the contact form reaches you, how many Google reviews you have right now, and whether the 4.8 and 85 on your site match your Google listing. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt doesn't block any AI crawler I tested. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked. It only blocks /admin and /auth.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 600 words of text and one clear heading (“Boulder City’s Rockabilly Beauty Lounge.”), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Gap
    Sitemap points to another domain

    The Sitemap line in robots.txt and every address inside sitemap.xml use rock-n-beauty-hub.lovable.app, not rocknbeautybc.com.

  • Gap
    Page addresses are written as “/”

    The canonical tag and og:url are a bare path such as “/about” on 8 of the 9 pages I opened. Only the community partners page uses a full address.

  • Note
    No llms.txt file

    rocknbeautybc.com/llms.txt returns a not-found page. This file is optional and I can't say whether any AI tool reads it.

  • Note
    Business markup is mostly complete

    The markup has your name, address, map position, phone, rating, a note that you are open by appointment only and four profile links. Its address, www.rocknbeautybc.com, did not load when I tested it, and the markup lists no Google Maps or review link.

  • Found
    Profiles are linked

    The pages link Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. I found no link to a Google listing in the page links, although the Contact page embeds a Google map.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.
